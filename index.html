IoT Healthcare System

Real-time monitoring of patients with metabolic syndrome

MetaCare IoT collects data from wearable and bedside sensors — temperature, glucose, heart rate, blood pressure, ECG and oxygen saturation — and shows it to doctors and patients in one clear dashboard. Abnormal readings trigger instant alerts so treatment can be adjusted before complications develop.
