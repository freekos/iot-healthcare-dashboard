Welcome
to Almaty

The biggest city in Kazakhstan and its student capital. The mountains start twenty minutes from the centre, canteens still serve a full lunch for 2,000 ₸, there is a coffee shop on every corner and a park to read in between classes. This guide collects the places that are actually useful for a student — with addresses, prices, altitude and a link to the map.

01 — Food

Where to Eat

Filling, cheap and close to the universities.

01

Kaganat

A self-service canteen chain loved by students: soups, plov, manty and a set lunch for about 2,000 ₸. Fast, no queue after 14:00.

Tole Bi St 59

↑ 790 m

Google Maps ↗
02

Navat

Central Asian cuisine — lagman, samsa, shashlik and big pots of tea. A bit pricier, good for a group dinner after exams.

Dostyk Ave 36

↑ 830 m

Google Maps ↗
03

Green Bazaar (Kök Bazar)

The main city market. Fresh fruit, dried apricots, baursaks and the cheapest street food in the centre. Try the samsa from the tandoor.

Zhibek Zholy St 53

03 — Study

Where to Study

Quiet halls and coworkings when the dorm is too loud.

07

Free reading halls, free Wi-Fi and complete silence. Bring your student ID to get a reader's card in ten minutes.

↑ 840 m

04 — Parks

Where to Relax

Parks and green places to switch off for an hour.

10

Panfilov Park

The central park of Almaty with the wooden Zenkov Cathedral. Shady alleys, benches and a five-minute walk to the Arbat.

Gogol St 40

↑ 780 m

Google Maps ↗
11

Botanical Garden

A huge quiet garden next to KazNU campus. Entry is about 500 ₸, almost no people on weekdays — perfect for a walk between lectures.

Timiryazev St 36D

↑ 900 m

Google Maps ↗
12

First President's Park

Fountains, wide alleys and the best view of the mountains in the city. Students come here to run, skate or just sit on the grass.

05 — Entertainment

What to Do

Weekend plans that fit a student budget.

13

Kok-Tobe

The hill above the city: cable car, the Beatles monument, rides and the TV tower. Go at sunset for the best photos.

Dostyk Ave 104B

↑ 1 100 m

Google Maps ↗
14

Medeu

The famous high-altitude ice rink 15 km from the centre. Skate in winter, hike to Shymbulak in summer — bus №12 goes right there.

Gornaya St 465

↑ 1 691 m

Google Maps ↗

Arbat (Zhibek Zholy)

The pedestrian street with street musicians, artists and cheap cafés. The usual meeting point for students on a Friday evening.
