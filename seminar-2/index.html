Welcome to Almaty

Almaty is the largest city in Kazakhstan and its student capital: more than 40 universities, mountains 20 minutes from the centre, cheap canteens, coffee shops on every corner and parks where you can read between classes. This guide collects places that are actually useful for a student — with addresses, prices and links to the map.

Where to Eat

Filling, cheap and close to the universities.

Kaganat

A self-service canteen chain loved by students: soups, plov, manty and a set lunch for about 2,000 ₸. Fast, no queue after 14:00.

Tole Bi St 59, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Navat

Central Asian cuisine — lagman, samsa, shashlik and big pots of tea. A bit pricier, good for a group dinner after exams.

Dostyk Ave 36, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Green Bazaar (Kök Bazar)

The main city market. Fresh fruit, dried apricots, baursaks and the cheapest street food in the centre. Try the samsa from the tandoor.

Zhibek Zholy St 53, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Where to Drink Coffee

Good Wi-Fi, sockets and a flat white under 1,500 ₸.

Nedelka Coffee

Local specialty-coffee chain. Small, quick and cheap — the place to grab a coffee before the first lecture.

Gogol St 92, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Coffeedelia

A classic Almaty café next to Panfilov Park. Big windows, long tables, laptops are welcome — many students write their coursework here.

Kabanbay Batyr St 66, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Bao Coffee & Bakery

Cosy bakery with croissants and cinnamon rolls. Quiet in the morning, so it works for breakfast plus an hour of reading.

Kurmangazy St 59, Almaty

Where to Study

Quiet halls and coworkings when the dorm is too loud.

National Library of Kazakhstan

Free reading halls, free Wi-Fi and complete silence. Bring your student ID to get a reader's card in ten minutes.

Abay Ave 14, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

SmArt.Point

Modern coworking with meeting rooms, printers and a student discount. Good for group projects and presentations.

Al-Farabi Ave 77/7, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Multispace

Coworking in the very centre, open late. Day passes, lockers and a kitchen — handy the night before a deadline.

Dostyk Ave 20, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Where to Relax

Parks and green places to switch off for an hour.

Panfilov Park

The central park of Almaty with the wooden Zenkov Cathedral. Shady alleys, benches and a five-minute walk to the Arbat.

Gogol St 40, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Botanical Garden

A huge quiet garden next to KazNU campus. Entry is about 500 ₸, almost no people on weekdays — perfect for a walk between lectures.

Timiryazev St 36D, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

First President's Park

Fountains, wide alleys and the best view of the mountains in the city. Students come here to run, skate or just sit on the grass.

Al-Farabi Ave / Navoi St, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

What to Do

Weekend plans that fit a student budget.

Kok-Tobe

The hill above the city: cable car, the Beatles monument, rides and the TV tower. Go at sunset for the best photos.

Dostyk Ave 104B, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Medeu

The famous high-altitude ice rink 15 km from the centre. Skate in winter, hike to Shymbulak in summer — bus №12 goes right there.

Gornaya St 465, Almaty

Open in Google Maps

Arbat (Zhibek Zholy)

The pedestrian street with street musicians, artists and cheap cafés. The usual meeting point for students on a Friday evening.

Zhibek Zholy St, between Panfilov St and Abylai Khan Ave

Open in Google Maps
